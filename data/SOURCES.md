# Data sources

Every real-world starting value in `data/*.yaml` is listed here with its source, or marked
`unsourced approximate` when it could not be verified. Tunables point here through their `source`
key.

## Status

M0 holds no real-world numbers. `countries.yaml` contains names, playable flags, archetypes, and
intended difficulty, all taken from `docs/DESIGN.md` (design intent, not data). Starting values
arrive in M1.
