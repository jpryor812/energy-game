# Data sources

Every real-world value in `data/*.yaml` names a source key below in its `source` field, or says
`Unsourced approximate` in its note. Country values are generated by
`scripts/data_prep/build_countries.py` from local copies of the files below, downloaded on
2026-11-07. The simulation itself never reads these files or the network.

## Sources

| Key | Publisher | Dataset and file | Vintage | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `energy_institute` | Energy Institute, *Statistical Review of World Energy*, via Our World in Data | `owid-energy-data.csv`, https://github.com/owid/energy-data | 2024 data | Primary energy use by source (TWh, substitution method, converted at 0.0036 EJ/TWh), fossil production, 2019-2024 change in use (BAU build mix, retirement rates), 2014-2024 energy-intensity trend, world totals and coverage shares |
| `gcb` | Global Carbon Project, *Global Carbon Budget*, via Our World in Data | `owid-co2-data.csv`, https://github.com/owid/co2-data | 2024 data | Fossil CO2 by fuel (emission factors, rest of world), cement/flaring/other CO2, land-use CO2 (2015-2024 mean) |
| `imf_weo` | IMF, *World Economic Outlook*, DataMapper API | `NGDPD` (GDP, current USD), `NGDP_RPCH` (real growth), `LP` (population), `GGXWDG_NGDP` (government gross debt, % GDP); https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/api/v1/ | Latest WEO available on 2026-11-07 | 2025 GDP and population, 2025-2030 growth, 2014-2024 growth (intensity trend), government debt |
| `wb_wdi` | World Bank, World Development Indicators API | `FI.RES.TOTL.CD` (total reserves incl. gold) | 2025 (Russia 2024) | FX reserves |
| `wb_ids` | World Bank, International Debt Statistics via WDI API | `DT.DOD.DPPG.CD` (public and publicly guaranteed external debt) | 2024 (Russia 2022) | Hard-currency debt |
| `un_wpp` | UN, *World Population Prospects 2024*, via Our World in Data | `population-with-un-projections.csv` (medium variant) | WPP 2024 | Population growth 2025-2050 (EU-27 summed from members) |
| `forster2025` | Forster et al. (2025), *Indicators of Global Climate Change 2024*, Earth System Science Data 17, 2641 | https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/17/2641/2025/ | 2024 | Starting warming (1.36 degC human-induced), warming rate (0.27 degC/decade) used to set the non-CO2 multiplier |
| `ipcc_ar6_wg1` | IPCC AR6 Working Group I, Summary for Policymakers | TCRE best estimate 1.65 degC per 1000 PgC | 2021 | Warming per tonne of CO2 |
| `iea_weo2025` | IEA, *World Energy Outlook 2025*, Current Policies Scenario (secondary coverage of the key findings) | https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-outlook-2025/overview-and-key-findings | 2025 | Calibration check only (docs/CALIBRATION.md), not an input |

## Derived and approximate values

| Value | How it was set |
| --- | --- |
| Nigeria energy mix | No fuel breakdown for Nigeria in the Energy Institute data. Fossil use = 2024 fuel CO2 / world emission factor; hydro and solar from 2023 electricity x world substitution factor; the rest of 2023 primary energy is other renewables. |
| Nigeria BAU build mix | Unsourced approximate (gas 0.5, solar 0.3, oil 0.2). |
| Saudi Arabia hard-currency debt | Unsourced approximate: half of government gross debt (not covered by International Debt Statistics). |
| Carbon prices | Unsourced approximate: headline 2025 price x share of emissions covered, rounded (EU ETS ~$80 x ~40% -> 32; UK, Canada ~20; China ETS ~$12 x ~40% -> 5; others 0-3). |
| Climate vulnerability | Unsourced approximate relative index (1 = typical). ND-GAIN was the intended source, but its download could not be fetched automatically; replace when available. |
| Fuel prices | Unsourced approximate 2025 averages (coal ~$105/t, Brent ~$69/bbl, gas between Henry Hub and LNG). |
| Long-run growth (`g0_end`) | Placeholder rule: min(g0_start, halfway from g0_start to 1.5%). |
| Tech costs, build times, lifespans, learning rates | Unsourced approximate, typical published ranges; see notes in `techs.yaml`. |
| Hard-currency issuers | USA, EU, Japan, UK, Canada and Australia borrow in their own currency: no hard-currency debt, FX reserves not tracked. |
